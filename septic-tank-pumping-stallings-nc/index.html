Why Stallings' Mixed Infrastructure Matters

Stallings' position between fully sewered Charlotte-area municipalities and more rural Union County towns means septic needs vary significantly by neighborhood.

  • Sewer coverage isn't uniform — Some sections of Stallings connect to municipal sewer while nearby streets remain on private septic. This patchwork means it's worth confirming your system type rather than assuming.
  • Older infill homes often predate sewer expansion — Homes built before Stallings' more recent utility expansion are more likely to be on original septic systems that are now decades old.
  • Border-area properties get less attention from some providers — Properties right at the Indian Trail or Matthews line sometimes fall through the cracks with providers who don't regularly cover that corridor. We service this area routinely.
  • A well-maintained system avoids costly emergency calls — Whether or not sewer expansion eventually reaches a given property, staying current on pumping avoids the far more expensive cost of an emergency backup.
