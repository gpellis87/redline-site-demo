What Union County Homeowners Should Know

Most Union County homes on private septic should have their tank pumped every 3–5 years depending on household size. Systems in older rural areas may have smaller tanks or aging drain fields that show symptoms sooner. If you notice slow drains, soggy ground near the drain field, or sewage odors inside or outside, those are early signs worth a professional look before the problem escalates. Redline can pump, inspect, or run a basic diagnostic during the same visit.
