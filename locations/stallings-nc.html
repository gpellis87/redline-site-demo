What Stallings Homeowners Should Know About Septic

Stallings has a mix of sewer-connected neighborhoods and private septic properties. If your home is outside public sewer coverage, keeping clear tank access and a consistent maintenance schedule is the easiest way to avoid backups.

  • Know whether you are on sewer or septic. If your utility bill has no sewer charge, your property may rely on a private system.
  • Small lots can make access important. Keeping lids, risers, and drive paths clear helps reduce service time and cost.
  • Older systems may need closer checks. Baffles, pipes, and distribution boxes can wear down before homeowners notice a full failure.
  • Emergency symptoms need quick action. Backups, sewage smells, and wet drain field areas should be addressed before household water use makes them worse.
