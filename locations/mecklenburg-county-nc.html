Private Septic Near Charlotte: What to Know

If your Mecklenburg County property is on private septic, most systems need pumping every 3–5 years regardless of whether you're noticing problems. Edge-area lots and older homes near the Union County border often have tanks or drain fields that haven't been serviced in years. When buying or selling a home with a septic system, a written inspection gives both parties a clear picture of the system's condition before closing day.
