What Cabarrus County Homeowners Should Know

Cabarrus County properties outside public sewer areas typically need their septic tank pumped every 3–5 years. Homes with garbage disposals or larger households may need service more frequently. If your drain field was installed in the 1980s or earlier, it may be approaching the end of its useful life. Early warning signs include wet spots in the yard, gurgling drains, or a sewage odor near the tank or field. Catching issues early is almost always less expensive than waiting for a full backup.
