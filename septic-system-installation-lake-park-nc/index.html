What Determines Your Lake Park System Design

Union County Environmental Health requires a site-specific soil evaluation for every new system, and the results determine what type of system your Lake Park property needs.

  • Soil texture and depth to restrictive layer — A licensed soil scientist evaluates the soil profile on your specific lot to determine whether a conventional gravity system will meet percolation requirements.
  • Lot size and layout — Available space for the drain field and required setbacks from wells, property lines, and structures affects where a system can be placed.
  • Seasonal water table — High water table conditions on some Lake Park lots can require a raised or alternative system design to maintain proper separation from groundwater.
  • Household size and water use — System sizing is based on projected daily water use, which is tied to the number of bedrooms and expected occupancy.
