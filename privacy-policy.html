Privacy Policy

Redline Site Services keeps this policy simple: we collect the information needed to answer service requests, maintain the website, and understand basic site activity.

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Information We Collect

When you contact Redline Site Services through this website, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, phone number, email address, service needed, message details, property address, and any other information you include in the form.

We may also receive basic technical information related to website visits, such as pages viewed, referring pages, device type, browser type, and approximate location based on standard web request data.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to service requests, provide estimates, schedule work, answer questions, follow up about active jobs, and maintain records related to customer communication.

We do not sell personal information.

Contact Forms and Spam Protection

Our contact form is processed through Web3Forms and may use hCaptcha or similar spam-prevention tools. These services help deliver your message to Redline and protect the form from automated abuse.

Analytics

We use Umami for privacy-focused website analytics. Analytics help us understand which pages are useful and how visitors find the site. Umami is used for aggregate site measurement, not advertising retargeting.

Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use advertising cookies or retargeting pixels on this site. Umami analytics is configured as a privacy-focused analytics tool and does not require cookies.

The accessibility controls on this site may use your browser's local storage to remember preferences such as larger text, higher contrast, or reduced motion. These settings stay in your browser so the site can remember your choices.

Third-party tools used for forms, spam protection, fonts, or security may use necessary cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Third-Party Services

This website may load or interact with third-party services, including:

  • Web3Forms for contact form submission
  • hCaptcha or similar form-protection tools
  • Umami for website analytics
  • Google Fonts for typography

These providers may process limited information needed to deliver their services.

How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, deliver contact form messages, prevent spam, or provide analytics. We may also share information when required by law or when needed to protect our business, customers, or website.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information submitted through the website. No website or online transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure, so avoid sending sensitive financial, medical, or other highly confidential information through the contact form.

Your Choices

You can contact us to ask about information you submitted through the website or to request that we delete a previous contact form submission when we no longer need it for business, legal, or recordkeeping purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions about this website, please reach out to Ellis Local at ellislocal.com.
