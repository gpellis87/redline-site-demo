Drainage Systems in Monroe & Union County

Union County properties deal with clay soil, heavy rain, wooded lots, low areas, and long driveways. The right drainage solution depends on where the water starts, where it needs to go, and what sits nearby. We look at the site before recommending a culvert, swale, grading correction, drain line, or excavation plan.

Culvert Pipe Installation

Culvert pipes help water pass under driveways, entrances, and access roads instead of washing them out. Common installations include driveway entrance culverts, creek-crossing pipes, and road drainage on rural lots. If your driveway washes out after heavy rain or water backs up at the entrance, a properly sized and graded culvert is usually the fix.

Drainage or septic issue? Wet yards can look like septic failure, drainage failure, or both. If the issue is near a tank, drain field, driveway, or low spot, contact Redline and we can help you sort out the cause.

How We Approach Drainage Work
