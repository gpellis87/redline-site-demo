What We Offer

Redline provides single-unit standard porta johns for short and long-term rental throughout Monroe, Union County, and the greater Charlotte area. Whether you need one unit for a weekend job or multiple units for an extended construction project, we can accommodate your timeline and site requirements.

Who We Serve

Redline's porta john rental service is designed for working job sites and events, not residential one-day parties. Our primary customers include:

  • Construction contractors — new home builds, additions, and site prep projects
  • Home builders and developers — subdivision and spec home construction throughout Union County
  • Remodelers and trade contractors — plumbers, framers, electricians, and general contractors who need facilities on active jobsites
  • Event organizers — outdoor events, festivals, and gatherings where permanent facilities are unavailable

Delivery & Service

We handle the entire process — delivery, placement, scheduled servicing, and final pickup. For longer rentals, we schedule regular service intervals to keep units clean and functional. For high-use sites or events, we can discuss more frequent servicing based on your anticipated use.

We ask that sites be accessible for our delivery truck. If your site has restricted access, narrow gates, or soft ground conditions, let us know when you call so we can plan accordingly.

Need same-day delivery? Call us directly at (704) 562-9922. Same-day availability depends on current inventory and your location — we'll give you a straight answer on the phone.

Pricing

Pricing depends on rental duration, quantity, and service frequency. We don't publish fixed rates because site conditions and schedules vary too much to be meaningful without context. Call or fill out the form and we'll give you a straightforward quote for your specific needs. No deposit required to reserve — you pay when service is complete.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a porta john rental?
We recommend booking at least 24–48 hours in advance for standard rentals. Same-day delivery is available depending on inventory and location — call to check availability.
How often are the units serviced?
Servicing frequency depends on your rental agreement. We typically service weekly for longer rentals, but we can schedule more frequent service for high-use sites.
Do you deliver to construction sites?
Yes. We deliver to active construction sites, job sites, renovation projects, and outdoor events throughout Monroe, Union County, and surrounding areas.
What areas do you serve for porta john rentals?
We deliver throughout Monroe, Indian Trail, Waxhaw, Wesley Chapel, Stallings, Concord, Kannapolis, Harrisburg, and surrounding Union County and Cabarrus County communities.
