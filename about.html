About Redline Site Services

Licensed, bonded, and insured septic company based in Monroe, NC — also known locally as Redline Septic. NC Septic License #9788, NC General Contractor License #109441.

Who We Are

Redline Site Services — sometimes called Redline Septic by longtime customers — is a licensed septic company based at 7322 Pleasant Hill Church Road in Monroe, NC. We've served Monroe and Union County homeowners for over 10 years, and have expanded to cover Cabarrus County, Mecklenburg County, Anson County, and the greater Charlotte metro area.

We handle the full range of septic work: pumping, cleaning, inspections, repair, emergency response, new system installation, tank replacement, drain field repair, drainage and excavation work, and porta john rental for job sites and events.

Licensing & Credentials

  • NC Septic License #9788 — authorizes septic system installation, repair, and pumping work under North Carolina state regulations.
  • NC General Contractor License #109441 — allows Redline to handle complete projects, including excavation and site work, under one contractor rather than coordinating multiple subcontractors.
  • Bonded & insured — every job is covered, giving homeowners and builders protection throughout the project.

Our Team & Equipment

Redline's crew is led on-site by Steven and a small team of local technicians who handle everything from routine pump-outs to full system installations. Customers consistently mention the same things in their reviews: on-time arrival, clear communication before work starts, and a clean job site when the crew leaves.

Our fleet includes vacuum pump trucks for tank pumping and cleaning, excavation equipment for installation and drain field work, and a porta john rental fleet for job sites and events. All work follows North Carolina septic code and, where applicable, Union County, Cabarrus County, Mecklenburg County, or Anson County Environmental Health permitting requirements.

Service Area

Redline serves Union County, Cabarrus County, Mecklenburg County, and Anson County, NC, including Monroe, Waxhaw, Indian Trail, Wesley Chapel, Stallings, Weddington, Marvin, Unionville, Mineral Springs, Lake Park, Matthews, Mint Hill, Huntersville, Charlotte, Concord, Kannapolis, Harrisburg, and Midland.
