What a Matthews Pump-Out Involves

Tank locating
We locate access lids using property records and probing on properties where landscaping has covered original access points.
Sludge & scum check
We measure sludge and scum levels before pumping to confirm service is actually needed and set an accurate next-interval recommendation.
Full vacuum evacuation
Liquid waste, scum, and loose sludge are removed via vacuum truck, with a quick baffle and tank-condition check included.
Older system inspection
Many outlying Matthews properties have systems dating to before sewer lines reached nearby streets. We note visible wear on baffles, risers, or lids.
Drain field visual check
We look for wet spots, odors, or surfacing wastewater near the drain field while we're on-site.
