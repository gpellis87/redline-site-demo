About Redline

Septic Solutions Done Right the First Time

Based in Monroe at 7322 Pleasant Hill Church Road, Redline Site Services is a licensed, bonded, and insured North Carolina septic company and NC General Contractor (License #109441) serving Union County, Cabarrus County, Mecklenburg County, and Anson County. Homeowners, builders, and property owners call us for routine maintenance, urgent backups, real estate inspections, system repairs, new installations, and replacement work.

When a system is open, our technicians check the parts that most often cause expensive failures: baffles, inlet and outlet tees, effluent filters, distribution boxes, drain field symptoms, risers, lids, and visible tank condition. For emergencies, call the 24/7 line at (704) 562-9922.
